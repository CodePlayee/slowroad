# Source-Blind Vegetation and Ground Comparison

Read only the assigned Q*/A.png and B.png pairs and this rubric. Source roles,
filenames, build notes, implementation and previous verdicts are deliberately
withheld. Do not inspect adjacent files, private keys or source code.

For each pair:

1. Select the visually stronger image: A, B or tie. Judge the image, not its
   presumed identity. Do not give credit for guessing which source is official.
2. Score each image 0-5 on grass structure, trees/shrubs/flowers, ground material,
   plant/ground contact, near-to-far vegetation transition, seasonal vegetation
   and ground treatment, and vegetation image cleanliness. Cite visible regions
   and concrete observations. The user explicitly limits changes to vegetation
   and ground. Do not score vehicle design/contact, asphalt/road markings, road
   shape, sky, global lighting or world composition as implementation targets.
3. Separately record camera/framing, time/weather, season, scene, vehicle and
   HUD comparability. Different layout is not a waiver for weak material or
   plant contact. Mark only truly unassessable dimensions N/A. Account for light
   and camera differences without making global lighting the optimization goal.
4. State whether a material visible quality gap remains. Identify the single
   largest remaining gap, its image region and a specific visual target.
5. Rank the three most consequential recurring gaps across the supplied set.

Identical opaque rectangles, if present, conceal UI in both images. Do not score
those pixels. No other image treatment, crop, color matching or retouching is
permitted. Static images cannot prove performance or temporal stability.

No browser, GPU, network, source inspection or communications with other judges.
Write only your assigned verdict, then stop. Do not declare whole-game acceptance
from a subset. Optional identity guesses may be written only after the quality
verdict is locked, and must never change it.
