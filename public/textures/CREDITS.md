## Meadow Turf V1

`meadow-turf-v1.png` is an original texture generated with the built-in image
generation tool on 2026-09-19, not a Poly Haven or Slow Roads asset. It is the
turf detail source in the active terrain material; `woodland-litter-v1.png`
supplies the forest floor. See `meadow-turf-v1.md` and `woodland-litter-v1.md`.
